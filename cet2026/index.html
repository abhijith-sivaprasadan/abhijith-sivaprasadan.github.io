Current direction

Entering nuclear by adding domain depth, not discarding existing engineering depth.

My current expertise is in thermodynamics, heat transfer, CFD/CHT, instrumentation, energy systems and numerical modelling. I am not presenting myself as a nuclear specialist yet.

What interests me is the transition path: how engineers from adjacent disciplines can become useful in nuclear research and industry through targeted nuclear coursework, supervised research and professional training.
