Why CET is relevant to me

I’m trying to cross into nuclear without throwing away the engineering I already know.

My background is not nuclear-specific yet. It is thermal engineering, experimentation, energy systems and modelling. The question I am exploring at CET is how engineers from adjacent disciplines can add the nuclear-specific layer — reactor physics, safety, thermal-hydraulics and domain practice — without starting their education from zero.
