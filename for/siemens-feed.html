01 · KTH master's thesis · Siemens Energy Finspång

High-temperature reducer CFD/CHT and instrumentation

In Siemens Energy's Fluid Dynamic Lab, I used steady-state ANSYS Fluent CFD/CHT to compare reducer geometries for a high-temperature dynamic pressure-sensor calibration rig. The work included mesh independence, Biot-number analysis, NI-DAQ/LabVIEW commissioning and heater-failure root cause analysis. The numerical findings were not experimentally validated after the heater failure.

02 · Independent research software · Thermal energy storage

TES Discharge Screen

A Python/Modelica and Pyomo/HiGHS screening workflow asks when state-dependent thermal-storage discharge changes annual process-heat decisions. Matched 2–12 h synthetic cases showed only 0.000%–0.038% isolated annual-cost differences; an FMU cross-check supports implementation consistency. This is not a design tool or a model validated against a real installation.

03 · Independent educational software · Steam and power

OpenSteamOpt

A synthetic two-boiler steam-and-power demonstrator links HP/MP headers, turbine and PRV operation to Pyomo/HiGHS scheduling. A Python shadow twin, Modelica/FMI path and Streamlit advisory interface share declared reference cases and balance checks. It is neither a commissioned plant twin nor an operational control system.

04 · Independent research software · Electricity, hydrogen and heat

PyNEXUS

A Pyomo/HiGHS screening model couples offshore wind, PEM electrolysis, hydrogen and optional heat recovery and storage. Recorded runs use a full year of fetched 2023 ERA5 wind, while prices and system assumptions remain illustrative. Annual wind-only cases were infeasible for the specified demand; results are not project-specific design predictions.

05 · Independent software toolkit · Industrial energy performance

Industrial Energy KPI Toolkit

An Excel, Python and Streamlit toolkit calculates industrial EnPIs, production/operating-hours baseline normalisation, anomaly flags and reports. The repository includes synthetic demonstrations and automated checks. Its regression is a screening baseline, not an IPMVP Option C measurement-and-verification protocol or evidence of savings at an operating plant.
