01 · KTH master's thesis · Siemens Energy Finspång

High-temperature reducer CFD/CHT and instrumentation

Investigated two reducer geometries for the Pulsatorn dynamic-pressure-sensor calibration rig using steady-state compressible ANSYS Fluent CFD and conjugate heat transfer. Compared pressure-loss and thermal-delivery behaviour using a three-level mesh study and Biot-number analysis. Commissioned NI-DAQ/LabVIEW measurement chains and documented a heater failure. The numerical design comparison was not experimentally validated; high-temperature validation was interrupted by the hardware failure.

02 · Independent research software · Thermal energy storage

TES Discharge Screen

Tests whether discharge-power limits informed by dynamic thermal behaviour change industrial process-heat storage decisions. Connects a packed-bed Python/Modelica model and FMI cross-checks to annual Pyomo/HiGHS optimisation and sensitivity analysis. The matched 2–12 h cases show a small isolated annual-cost effect of roughly 0.000%–0.038%. This is a screening framework with synthetic loads and prices, not a design tool or a model validated against a real storage installation.

03 · Independent educational software · Steam and power

OpenSteamOpt

Models a two-boiler HP/MP steam network with a back-pressure turbine, pressure-reducing valve and grid exchange. A Pyomo/HiGHS scheduling model connects to a Python shadow twin, a Modelica/FMI path and a local Streamlit advisory interface. Reference runs include explicit steam/power balance checks and synthetic cost comparisons. This is a synthetic, advisory-only demonstrator—not a commissioned plant twin, ABB product or operational control system.

04 · Independent research software · Electricity, hydrogen and heat

PyNEXUS

Couples offshore wind, PEM electrolysis, hydrogen demand and optional storage, grid exchange and waste-heat recovery in a Pyomo/HiGHS dispatch model. A full year of fetched 2023 ERA5 wind was used in recorded runs, with independent mass/energy and objective checks. Annual wind-only cases were infeasible under the stated assumptions; the reported grid-and-storage scenario still left unmet demand. It remains a screening model: demand, capacities and interim electricity prices are illustrative rather than project-specific.

05 · Independent software toolkit · Industrial energy performance

Industrial Energy KPI Toolkit

Combines an Excel input template, a Python runner and a Streamlit workflow for industrial EnPIs, baseline normalisation, anomaly flags and management reports. The repository provides synthetic demonstration data, test cases and example outputs. Its regression baseline is a screening method, not an IPMVP Option C measurement-and-verification protocol. Real sites would require metering, baseline and threshold assessment before use.
