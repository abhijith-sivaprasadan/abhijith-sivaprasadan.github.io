01 / WORKING METHOD

How I approach this type of problem.

Transferable engineering habits, not claims that all methods have been validated in every sector.

  1. Set metering and energy-accounting boundaries
  2. Normalise energy against production and drivers
  3. Screen heat-recovery, HTHP or storage feasibility
  4. Quantify uncertainty before investment conclusions

A useful 30-second introduction: Open with EnPIs and the measurement boundary, not a promised percentage saving. Explain why matched useful heat matters more than nominal storage size. Show an explicit feasibility failure when compressor or delivery constraints are unsupported.
