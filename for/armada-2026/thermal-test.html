01 / WORKING METHOD

How I approach this type of problem.

Transferable engineering habits, not claims that all methods have been validated in every sector.

  1. Define geometry, boundary conditions and measurement objectives
  2. Commission and check sensor / acquisition chain
  3. Compare numerical cases and error sensitivity
  4. Document failure, limitations and next test

A useful 30-second introduction: Start with the physical test objective, not a screenshot from Fluent. Explain what I personally instrumented or modelled and how I checked it. Distinguish observed rig behavior from numerical reducer conclusions.
