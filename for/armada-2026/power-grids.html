01 / WORKING METHOD

How I approach this type of problem.

Transferable engineering habits, not claims that all methods have been validated in every sector.

  1. Define grid or energy service and constraints
  2. Track source data, scenario assumptions and provenance
  3. Solve dispatch, power-flow or screening model
  4. Stress-test feasibility and explain residual limits

A useful 30-second introduction: Lead with what is and is not grounded in real grid data. Show the problem formulation, not an unsupported network map. Discuss source coverage and sensitivity before claiming decisions.
