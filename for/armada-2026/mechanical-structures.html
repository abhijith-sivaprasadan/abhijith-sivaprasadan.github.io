01 / WORKING METHOD

How I approach this type of problem.

Transferable engineering habits, not claims that all methods have been validated in every sector.

  1. Define function, geometry and realistic load cases
  2. Set material and boundary assumptions
  3. Check mesh, balance and reaction quantities
  4. State what certification and measured evidence still require

A useful 30-second introduction: Show a transparent load case instead of calling an educational FEA a qualified design. Connect numerical checks to a test plan or engineering decision. Be explicit about the sector-specific standards I would need to learn.
