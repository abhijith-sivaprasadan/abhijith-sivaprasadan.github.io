01 / WORKING METHOD

How I approach this type of problem.

Transferable engineering habits, not claims that all methods have been validated in every sector.

  1. Translate client question into an explicit work scope
  2. Check input provenance, units and missing data
  3. Build and independently cross-check a model or tool
  4. Deliver a concise decision note with uncertainties

A useful 30-second introduction: Lead with one deliverable and a defensible method, not a long list of tools. Show how I would work under a specialist engineer's review. Offer a focused work sample for thermal, energy or software assignments.
