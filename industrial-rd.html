Three proof lines

From a baseline, to a decision, to a tool

The three cases chain: understand what an industrial site consumes and why, choose between supply options, then package the logic so someone else can run it.

STAGE 1 · MAP

Industrial energy performance mapping

BOUNDARY
Electrical utilities and compressed air as industrial electrification pathways.
METHOD
KPI/EnPI design · load-driver logic · metering-gap assessment · deviation detection · regulatory comparison
RESIDUAL
Desk-based method development. No field implementation, commissioning or measured savings.
→ STAGE 2 · DECIDE

Multi-objective heat-source dispatch

BOUNDARY
CHP, heat pump, geothermal and storage, coupled to electricity price signals.
METHOD
Python · PuLP · stepwise MILP built from first principles · cost and emissions objectives
RESIDUAL
Scenario or prototype study; no live operational validation is claimed.
→ STAGE 3 · PACKAGE

Industrial energy KPI toolkit

BOUNDARY
Baseline normalisation, KPI reporting, anomaly flags and action tracking.
METHOD
Python · Streamlit · CLI · automated tests · PDF report generation · CI
RESIDUAL
Proof-of-concept tool, not a deployed plant system.
What the work shows

Four kinds of industrial R&D signal

Process and energy modelling
Energy balance, load-driver mapping, baseline normalisation, scenario modelling and technology comparison across electrical utilities, compressed air, heating and cooling.
Optimisation
Linear and mixed-integer dispatch models, objective-function design, constraint handling and cost-emissions trade-offs. Growth area: automatic control and MATLAB/Simulink.
Proof-of-concept tools
Reproducible tools with CLI interfaces, front ends, tests and reports, built for handover rather than one-off exploration.
Technical communication
Formal reports, regulatory comparison, documented assumptions and a clear route from model output to industrial action.
Open boundary
Where the work goes next
Industrial electrification beyond system-level energy modelling.
Electrothermal process heating, thermal storage, cooling integration, electrochemical process routes, control-oriented modelling and experimental validation. The current strength is structured modelling and tool-building; the best fit is an environment where that discipline meets advanced simulation and experimental facilities.
