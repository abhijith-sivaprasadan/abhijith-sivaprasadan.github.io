Working style

Three habits that show in every case

01
Validate assumptions early
Clear boundary conditions, documented limitations and sanity checks before a model or a test setup is scaled.
02
Connect analysis to hardware
Keep instrumentation, test constraints and model interpretation tied together instead of treating simulation as a standalone exercise.
03
Move between disciplines
Switch between thermal-fluid engineering, energy systems and software while holding the same standard of evidence and traceability.
