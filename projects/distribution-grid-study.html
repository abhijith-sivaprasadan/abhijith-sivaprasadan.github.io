KTH MJ2505 · Pandapower · CIGRE MV · Academic archive

Distribution Grid Study: Archived Coursework and Reproduction Status

Academic coursework exploring CIGRE medium-voltage power flow and time-varying demand. The root PDF is a course-provided assignment brief, not a submitted results report. Historical Step 1 and Step 2 notebooks are preserved on a separate branch. The public repository is not a verified reproducible software release.

Coursework archive Reproduction plan

What the preserved code demonstrates

  • Step 1: A historical notebook creates the built-in CIGRE MV example and calls pandapower.runpp, inspecting bus voltages and line loading.
  • Step 2: A separate notebook constructs a 24-hour load profile and calls pandapower's time-series runner, with output logging configured for bus voltage and line loading.
  • Engineering context: Load-flow interpretation, the role of time-dependent demand, and the importance of checking numerical output provenance.

The notebooks are preserved on the historical step_2 branch. They have not yet been cleanly rerun, and no submitted student results report was identified in the public repository. The linked PDF documents the assignment tasks, not the results.

What is not independently verified

  • The report's numerical loading values, including previously displayed 110.6% line and 104.4% transformer loading figures.
  • A complete N-1 contingency implementation and quantitative EV/PV/storage optimisation chain in the publicly reviewed notebooks.
  • The claimed 348-profile EV dataset, including its inputs, redistribution permissions and relation to final reported results.
  • Correspondence between exported spreadsheets and the full hour-by-hour simulation.

The Step 2 notebook also builds post-run hourly result tables by repeatedly reading net.res_bus and net.res_line after the time-series loop. Those objects reflect the latest network state, not automatically the state at each historical time step. The separately configured OutputWriter may contain time-indexed results, but this needs verification.

Evidence and provenance

How to interpret this project

This is a record of university-level grid-analysis work, not currently evidence of a deployable pandapower tool or independently validated contingency results. The course assignment brief is retained for task context, not as proof of completed calculations, while the provenance note records unresolved team and input-licensing questions. The reproduction plan sets out the remaining checks.

Current classification: archived coursework artifacts and instructions; neither complete implementation nor numerical results are independently evidenced. Future reconstructed code must be separately identified and tested before it is cited as new reproducible evidence.
