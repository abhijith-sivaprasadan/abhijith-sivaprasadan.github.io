CarnotBatteryLab

A verification-first research platform for industrial high-temperature heat pumps (HTHP), thermal energy storage (TES), flexible process heat and possible Carnot-battery architectures. The project is intended to expose modelling assumptions and hardware feasibility before any architecture or investment recommendation.

Heat pumps / thermal storagePython · scientific methodsEnergy and exergy balancesIndependent research
Scientific status: research foundation, not a validated system. The repository documents v0.1.0 core contracts, energy/exergy and hydraulic primitives, test/CI workflows, and subsequent unvalidated screening and charging-model increments. It does not establish a complete experimentally validated heat-pump/storage/heat-delivery cycle. The earlier A/B/C architecture ranking was withdrawn in the 15 November 2026 audit; it must not be quoted as a valid design ranking.

What is implemented — and what is not

01 / Reproducible foundation

Cases, identities and checks

Typed input validation, dimensionally explicit energy/exergy identities, storage hydraulic primitives, unit-aware cost arithmetic, provenance manifests, tests and CI. These verify parts of the implementation rather than industrial prediction accuracy.

02 / Real-fluid research screen

R718 cycle and finite heat exchangers

R718 water-vapour screening with heat-exchanger approach/UA checks, temperature and flow constraints and guarded compressor-stage assumptions. Illustrative numerical controls are not manufacturer qualification.

03 / Coupled charging model

Storage state changes required temperature

A 1-D two-temperature gas/rock packed-bed charging model includes state-dependent condenser conditions, transient thermal behavior, Ergun pressure loss and blower electricity. This is charging-side research, not a completed discharge or cyclic plant model.

04 / Blocked claims

Hardware and experimental validation

The evidence does not yet support a qualified high-lift compressor for the original source envelope, independently measured packed-bed breakthrough, matched useful process-heat discharge or electricity-to-electricity round-trip performance.

Scientific comparison contract

A useful result must satisfy the same delivered energy service, temperature window, time basis and operating constraints across architectures. Infeasible compressor or storage states are rejected—not hidden inside a worse efficiency or cost metric.

Next evidence gates

  1. Establish operating-envelope-supported compressor or alternative cycle architecture; clearly separate source temperatures and vendor equipment evidence.
  2. Independently reproduce packed-bed breakthrough, pressure loss and transient temperatures using measured data with documented uncertainty.
  3. Develop and verify discharge, actual process-heat delivery and repeatable cyclic boundary conditions.
  4. Only then run matched-service cost, dispatch and architecture comparisons with source-year traceability.

These are research objectives, not completed results. The precise status and limitations are maintained in the source repository rather than implied from this short project page.
