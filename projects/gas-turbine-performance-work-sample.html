01 / Engineering problem

A test result is only meaningful against the right reference.

Installed gas-turbine performance depends on intake temperature and pressure, filter pressure loss, exhaust backpressure, turbine firing limits, fuel energy and mechanical/electrical boundaries. The task is to distinguish changes in operating conditions from changes in machine health, while maintaining a controlled equipment identity.

Three distinct questions

Predict. How does a fixed generic machine respond to changed site conditions? Evaluate thermodynamic station states and power/heat rate with a documented reference.

Assess. Do actual generator power, voltage, phase currents and power factor agree under the measured meter conventions?

Decide. Would a contractual pass-off require additional calibrated measurements, correction-model uncertainty and guaranteed performance terms? Yes. That assessment remains unperformed here.

What the browser computes

For fixed pressure ratio πc, compressor efficiency ηc and turbine efficiency ηt, two constant-property ideal-gas station relations are evaluated:

T₂ = T₁ [1 + (πc^((γair−1)/γair) − 1)/ηc] T₄ = T₃ [1 − ηt (1 − (P₄/P₃)^((γgas−1)/γgas))] ṁair = ṁref (P₁/Pref) √(Tref/T₁) · CPower = Pref,model/Psite,model · CHR = HRref,model/HRsite,model

Compressor work is ṁaircp,air(T₂−T₁); turbine work is (ṁair+ṁfuel)cp,gas(T₃−T₄). Burner fuel demand uses a simplified fixed-LHV sensible-enthalpy balance; mechanical/generator efficiencies and auxiliary fraction are fixed. These browser equations are a pedagogical independent cross-section and are not numerically identified against a particular turbine. For a complete Fortran implementation and verified boundary-correction workflow, follow the source-linked site model methodology.

04 / From measured test to production pass-off · methodology

A correction factor is not an acceptance decision.

A production test requires a true source-identified engine/test interval, known flanges and fuel basis, calibrated instruments, independently defined customer guarantee and model-form uncertainty. ThermoTwin-F implements the relevant correction-factor and instrument-covariance mathematics, but no actual production pass-off or contractual result has been performed.

HRsite [kJ/kWh] = 3600 × (ṁfuel [kg/s] × LHV [J/kg] / 10⁶) / Psite [MW] [Pcorrected, HRcorrected] = [CP × Psite, CHR × HRsite] Σcorrected = J (Σprecision + Σcalibration) Jᵀ · J = ∂[Pcorrected, HRcorrected]/∂[Psite, ṁfuel, LHV]
