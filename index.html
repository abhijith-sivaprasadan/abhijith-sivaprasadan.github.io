Methodology & equations
  • Isentropic relations: Anderson, Modern Compressible Flow, Ch.3 — staticTemperatureFromTotal, staticToTotalPressure in scripts/physics/gas-dynamics.js
  • Adiabatic wall T: Schlichting, Boundary-Layer Theory Ch.15; r = Pr^(1/3) for turbulent BL — adiabaticWallTemperature
  • 1-D resistance network: Incropera & DeWitt Ch.3 — thermalResistanceCircuit in scripts/physics/heat-transfer.js
  • Biot: Bi = h·L_c/k — biotNumber; thesis-reported band 0.003–0.004 quoted from TRITA-ITM-EX 2026:14
  • Reynolds: ideal-gas closure ρ = p/(R·T); μ via Sutherland at 673 K — reynoldsNumber

Methodology & equations

Methalox regenerative nozzle cooling

Autonomous burn cycle

A continuous methalox combustion plume passes through a regeneratively cooled de-Laval nozzle. Chamber thermochemistry is from a polynomial fit to NASA CEA across O/F 2.5–4.5 and Pc 5–30 MPa. Wall heat transfer uses the full Bartz equation with the σ transport-property correction. Coke deposition follows Arrhenius kinetics on the local wall temperature.

Realistic methalox model — chamber state from NASA CEA polynomial fit, full Bartz with σ correction, Arrhenius coke kinetics, T-dependent kCu, mass-conservation coolant closure. All formulas in scripts/physics/. Bartz 1965 Bartz σ (TN D-7973) Gordon & McBride CEA Hayhurst & Lawrence 1992

Methodology & equations
  • Chamber thermochemistry: methaloxChamberState(P_c, OF) returns T_c, γ, R_s, cp, Pr, μ, c* — polynomial fit to NASA CEA (Gordon & McBride, RP-1311) calibrated to T_c = 3550 K at OF = 3.6 / P_c = 10 MPa. scripts/physics/combustion.js
  • Choked mass flow: ṁ = P_c · A_t / c* (Sutton & Biblarz Eq. 3-24)
  • Vacuum Isp: Isp_vac = c* · C_F_vac / g₀; C_F from area-ratio inversion (Newton iteration)
  • Full Bartz with σ: h_g* = (0.026/D_t^0.2)·(μ^0.2·cp/Pr^0.6)·(P_c/c*)^0.8·(D_t/r_c)^0.1·σ; σ = 1/[(½(T_w/T_c)(1+(γ−1)/2·M²) + ½)^0.68 · (1+(γ−1)/2·M²)^0.12] (NASA TN D-7973)
  • Local Bartz scaling: h_g(x) = h_g* · (A_t/A(x))^0.9 · σ(x)/σ_throat
  • Wall T iteration: Picard loop (4 passes per axial station) for T_w — h_g depends on T_w via σ; T_w depends on q; q depends on h_g
  • T-dependent k_Cu: 400 W/mK at 300 K → 330 at 800 K → 290 at 1100 K (NIST/CRC fits)
  • Coolant ΔT closure: ΔT = ∫q″·dA / (ṁ_CH₄·cp_CH₄), with ṁ_CH₄ = ṁ_total/(1+OF) (mass conservation)
  • Coke kinetics: dδ/dt = A·exp(−Eₐ/RT_w), Eₐ = 245 kJ/mol (Hayhurst & Lawrence, Combust. Flame 91:67-79, 1992). Pre-exponential calibrated for ~30 s browser playback; real CH₄ coking is ~100× slower at the same T_w. The Arrhenius temperature sensitivity (exponent) is the physically meaningful part.
  • Burnout limit: T_burnout = 1170 K (OFHC copper, AMS 4500)
  • Conductance health: R_clean / R_total — coke layer's share of the thermal-resistance network

Methodology & equations
  • EnPI: ISO 50006:2014 §5; EnPI = E_purchased / production — energyPerformanceIndicator
  • SEC: ISO 50001:2018 Annex A — specificEnergyConsumption
  • Carnot HP COP: COP = η_2nd·T_sink/(T_sink − T_source); η_2nd = 0.50 (Arpagaus et al., RSER 2018); T_source = 10 °C ambient — heatPumpCarnotCOP
  • Capacity-limited merit order: HP up to 2.0 MW, EB trim up to 1.8 MW, gas balance — dispatchMeritOrder
  • Heat-recovery curve: η_HR = 0.18 + 0.04·load (effectiveness floor + load slope) — heatRecoveryEffectiveness
  • Emissions: weighted (E_el·g_grid + E_gas·g_gas)/production — emissionsIntensity
  • Scopes: GHG Protocol Corporate Standard — scopeSplit
  • MAC: ΔOPEX_annual / ΔCO₂_avoided; el = 850 SEK/MWh, gas = 350 SEK/MWh_LHV, 7200 h/y — marginalAbatementCost
  • Primary energy: E_primary = PEF·E_final; PEF_el = 1.9 (EU EED) — toPrimaryEnergy
  • Boundaries: Q_dem = 5.2 MW × load; production = 10 u/h × load; parasitic = 320 kW × load
