RESEARCH STATEMENT · FIRST PERSON
The one place the site says "I"

Why I check by hand

I came to simulation from backend software, where a wrong answer fails silently unless a test catches it. Simulation has the same failure mode. So before I trust a model I work the governing equations by hand, step by step, and compare. The plot is the last thing I believe, not the first.

That habit is the reason this site lists limits next to results. It is also why I keep building small open tools that check other people's models, and my own.
